About Dealerships

Welcome to Best Cars Dealership, a fictional national car retailer in North America. Our educational portal brings branch information and customer feedback together in one place. Visitors can find locations across the United States, compare reviews, and make informed decisions before visiting.

Our demo combines a responsive React interface with Django account and inventory features, plus services for dealership data, customer reviews, and review sentiment.

This educational project uses sample dealership and vehicle records.
